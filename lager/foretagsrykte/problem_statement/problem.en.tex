\problemname{Company Reputation}
You have just started a company.
You know that what the company does is not very popular ---
each day $i$ during the next $n$ days you expect your reputation to worsen by some given value $r_i$.
You will then also lose as much money as your reputation is bad.
During the night you have the opportunity to reset your reputation by renaming the company.
This can be done any number of times, but costs a fixed sum $k$.

What is the minimum loss you can achieve?

\section*{Input}
The first line contains two integers $n$ and $k$ ($2 \leq n \leq 4 \cdot 10^5$, $0 \leq k \leq 2 \cdot 10^9$).
The second line contains $n$ integers $r_i$ ($1 \le r_i \le 10^7$) --- how much your reputation will worsen during day $i$.

\section*{Output}
Output one integer, the minimum loss you can achieve.

\section*{Scoring}
Your solution will be tested on a set of test groups. To earn points for a group, you must pass all test cases in that group.

\noindent
\begin{tabular}{| l | l | p{12cm} |}
  \hline
  \textbf{Group} & \textbf{Points} & \textbf{Constraints} \\ \hline
  $1$    & $12$      & $2 \le n \le 20$ \\ \hline
  $2$    & $17$      & $2 \le n \le 1000$ \\ \hline
  $3$    & $25$      & $k \le 1000$, $r_i \le 1000$  \\ \hline
  $4$    & $23$      & All $r_i$ are the same \\ \hline
  $5$    & $23$      & No additional constraints. \\ \hline
\end{tabular}

\section*{Explanation of Sample 1}
Here it is optimal to rename three times: during the first, second, and third night.

\section*{Explanation of Sample 2}
Here it is optimal to rename once, during the night between day 15 and day 16.
